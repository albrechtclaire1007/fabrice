II · La vostra identità

Fabrice Laurent

You are a discerning French collector known for a formidable cellar and for being very difficult to impress. You have spent years building a reputation for judgement, rarity and impeccable provenance.

You are confident, polished and proud of your expertise. You dislike being challenged publicly, but you are far too sophisticated to appear rattled. Tonight, however, you have more at stake than anyone else realises.

Perché siete qui

You have been invited as a serious wine collector whose opinion carries weight. The rediscovery of a genuine Riserva 1947 should have been fascinating to you. Instead, it threatens everything you have tried to keep buried.

Come vestirsi

Refined French collector in 1968: dark or richly toned tailoring, crisp shirt, discreet luxury and immaculate details. You should look like someone accustomed to private cellars, auctions and expensive dinners.

IV · In confidenza

Da leggere con discrezione

CONFIDENTIAL: YOU ARE THE MURDERER.

You knowingly authenticated and brokered bottles presented as Antonelli–Mastropietro Riserva 1947 even though you knew their provenance was false. The scheme relied partly on an old preliminary label proof that made the bottles appear convincing. Patrizio Moretti has been investigating those sales and is close to proving your involvement.

The newly rediscovered bottle is dangerous to you because it is an uncontested, estate-held reference bottle. If Patrizio compares it with the suspicious bottles and publishes what he has found, your reputation—and potentially much more—will collapse. During aperitivo, you will have to murder Patrizio within the game before he can expose you.

Regole per voi

You may lie about your motives, suspicions and movements. You may claim you trusted a seller’s provenance. You may not invent physical evidence, destroy or hide props, claim another player told you something they did not, or refuse a direct reveal instruction later in the evening.

V · Prima di cena

Durante l’aperitivo

I

Have a normal conversation about rare wine with Stefano. Do not behave suspiciously from the start.

II

Ask Patrizio, as casually as possible, what exactly he is researching and whether he intends to publish anything soon.

III

Speak with Valérie or Lolita about an ordinary subject so that your evening does not revolve entirely around Patrizio.

IV

Only after at least 15 minutes of aperitivo and after completing at least two of the objectives above: get Patrizio alone naturally. Quietly tell him: “Your character has been killed. Go to the bathroom now and follow the envelope marked PATRIZIO.” Then return to the others and behave normally. Do not touch him, give him anything, mime violence or alter any real food or drink.

La cena seguirà quando tutti saranno arrivati.
